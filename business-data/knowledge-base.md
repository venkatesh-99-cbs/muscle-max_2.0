## 3. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### Who can use Muscle Max supplements?
Most Muscle Max products are formulated for healthy adults engaged in regular training or looking to support general nutrition. Specific recommendations vary by product — check the individual product's 'who should use' and 'precautions' notes, or ask about a specific product by name.

### Is there an age limit for supplements?
As a general guideline, sports-performance products (protein powders, pre-workout, creatine, amino acids) are formulated for adults 18+ and are not recommended for minors without a doctor's guidance. Basic health & nutrition items (like multivitamins) may have age-appropriate versions, but our standard products are also formulated for adults — a parent/guardian should consult a pediatrician before giving any supplement to a minor.

### How do I use a supplement safely?
Always follow the serving size printed on the product label — more is not better. Introduce one new supplement at a time so you can tell what's causing any reaction. Stay well hydrated, especially with creatine and pre-workout. If you're pregnant, nursing, taking medication, or managing a medical condition, check with a doctor before starting any new supplement.

### Are there side effects I should know about?
Effects vary by product. Stimulant-containing items (like pre-workout) can affect sleep or cause jitteriness if taken late in the day or in excess. Beta-alanine commonly causes a harmless tingling sensation. High-mineral or vitamin products can cause digestive upset if you exceed the labeled dose. Ask about a specific product for its individual precautions, or consult a doctor if you have concerns.

### Can I take multiple supplements together?
Many customers combine products from different categories (e.g. whey protein + creatine, or a multivitamin + omega-3). Be mindful of overlapping ingredients across products (for example, several products may each contain caffeine) so you don't unintentionally exceed a safe total. If you're combining several supplements or are on medication, it's worth checking with a doctor or pharmacist first.

### Can I use these products while pregnant or nursing?
We recommend consulting a doctor before using any supplement while pregnant or nursing — this applies across our whole range, not just one product.

### Do your products contain common allergens?
Several protein products contain milk (dairy), and some items may contain soy or be produced in facilities that also process nuts or gluten-containing ingredients. Always check the specific product's allergen information before purchasing if you have an allergy.
